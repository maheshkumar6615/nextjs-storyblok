**Measurable Business Impact and Delivery:**

- Completed the migration through an automated approach with **zero downtime**.
- Reduced manual migration activities through reusable automation tools.
- Improved migration accuracy through automated page validation and asset-reference updates.
- Reduced operational risks associated with asset references, asset approvals, and accidental asset deletion.
- Established reusable migration tooling and processes to accelerate subsequent migration waves.

This initiative transformed a complex migration process into a more automated, repeatable, and reliable migration framework while ensuring business continuity through zero-downtime delivery.
